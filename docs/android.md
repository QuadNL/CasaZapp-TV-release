# The Android app

One APK for everything: Google TV, Chromecast with Google TV, Android TV boxes, phones and
tablets. It needs Android 6 or newer. The app picks the TV or phone layout by itself.

- **[Installing](android-install.md):** on a phone, or on a TV with Downloader. After that the app
  updates itself.
- **[Connecting](android-connect.md)** to your server, with a code you accept once.
- **[Without a server](android-local.md):** a playlist on the device itself.
- **[The remote](android-remote.md):** what the buttons do in the player.
